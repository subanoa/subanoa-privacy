Description

Material Doubt: The Motive Ledger is a detective game built on evidence chains. Study a scene, log what it holds, pull the records that contradict a story, interview the people who told it, and commit to a conclusion only when the file can carry it.

Accuse the wrong person and your Case Integrity pays for it. Sometimes the honest answer is that the evidence is insufficient.

Four ways to work a case

  • Investigations. Ten full case files: scene, evidence locker, interviews, deduction board and verdict.
  • Snap Cases. Sixteen photographs. One image, several suspects: solve it on sight, then point at the detail that proves it.
  • Manhunts. Six serial cases on a corkboard. Earn traits, strike suspects, work the city map and make the arrest.
  • Material Doubt: Cold Files. A true-crime channel of eight dramatized historical cases plus a bonus tape, narrated, with subtitles.

Features

  • Every solution is provable from evidence the player can see or unlock
  • Nobody is guilty because of how they look, where they come from or what they do for a living
  • Case Integrity replaces lives: guessing costs you, reasoning does not
  • Ten ranks, Merit and five competencies that grow from the work you actually do
  • Over seven hours of narration across five voices, with subtitles
  • Plays offline; text scaling, reduced motion and clues that never depend on colour alone

Free browser edition

The free browser edition contains 4 investigations, 5 snap cases, 1 full manhunt and every Cold Files broadcast. Progress does not carry to the Windows edition.

How it was made

Material Doubt uses generative and AI-assisted tools for selected graphics, narration, text/dialogue, and code. Narration uses Azure neural text-to-speech. No generative AI runs during gameplay. Music and sound effects are synthesized in code.

About the developer

Subanoa is an independent developer making detective games about reasoning rather than guessing. Material Doubt: The Motive Ledger is its first release.
