Information saved on your device, in every edition

The game saves these on the device you play on:

  • your progress: cases, snap cases, manhunts and broadcasts opened or solved, unfinished investigations, grades, Merit, rank and competencies;
  • your settings, such as text size, sound, narration, reduced motion and orientation;
  • what you own, so the game knows what to unlock;
  • on Windows, the size and position of the window;
  • on Google Play, the count and timing of ads shown, so the game can limit how often they appear.

We do not receive this information and cannot retrieve it for you. There is no cloud save. You can erase your progress with Reset all progress in Settings, by clearing the site's data in your browser, or by uninstalling the game.

What the game's own code does not do, in any edition

  • It has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or other contact details.
  • It has no analytics or crash reporting of its own.
  • It does not ask for your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.
  • It has no chat or user-generated content.
  • It does not contact any AI service while you play. All text, art and narration ship with the game.

Free browser edition and paid Windows edition (itch.io)

These editions contain no advertising and no AdMob or RevenueCat software. While you play, they load only the game's own files. The links to the game's store page, tip jar and this policy open only when you choose them. The Support button opens your own email app; nothing is sent unless you send it.

itch.io hosts these editions and processes any purchase or tip. We do not see your payment details. itch.io's handling of your information, including its server logs and your purchase, is covered by the itch.io privacy policy.

Google Play edition

This edition uses the third-party services below. They run on your device and connect to their own servers. They are not used in the itch.io editions.

If you have Android device backup turned on, Android may copy this game's saved data to your own Google backup. That is a feature of your device; we do not receive that data.

Advertising: Google AdMob

The free Google Play edition can show two kinds of ad: full-screen interstitial ads after a completed case, and rewarded ads you choose to watch to open the Daily Sealed Snap.

Google's published disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK says it automatically collects:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • app activity, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • app information and performance data, such as launch time and diagnostic information;
  • device and other identifiers: your Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers tied to accounts signed in on the device.

Google says this data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention and is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Buying Ad-Free Mode or the Full Case File stops automatic ads. The ad software remains part of the Google Play app, so it may still start and contact Google.

Consent: Google User Messaging Platform

Before any ad is requested, Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether a consent message applies where you are. If it does, the game shows Google's consent form and requests ads only if the result allows it. Your choice is stored on your device. Where Google requires a way to change that choice, a privacy options button appears in Settings.

Your advertising ID

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Some Android versions place this under Privacy, then Ads.

Purchases: Google Play billing and RevenueCat

Google Play processes payment for Ad-Free Mode and the Full Case File. We never see your payment details. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and to restore them. RevenueCat acts as our data processor. The game identifies you to RevenueCat only by a random, anonymous ID generated on your device; it sends no name or email address. RevenueCat processes that ID, your device type and operating system, the time it last saw the app, and your purchase information, including the Google purchase token and what you bought. We can view these purchase records, without your name or contact details, in RevenueCat's dashboard. Details are in the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Sale of information and legal bases

We do not sell information to anyone for money. In the Google Play edition, data used for advertising may count as a "sale" or "sharing" under some US state laws. Where those laws apply, your choices are offered through Google's privacy options described above.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent for personalized advertising where consent is required, on performing our contract with you for purchases, and on legitimate interests in securing and operating the game.

How long information is kept

Information on your device stays until you erase it. Google and RevenueCat keep the information they process according to their own policies. If you email us, we keep your message for as long as we need it to help you and to keep a record of the request.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Information saved on your device is already in your control. To ask us to delete a RevenueCat purchase record, email Subantech@gmail.com and include your Google Play order number so we can look for it. Deleting that record can affect restoring a purchase. For information held by Google or itch.io, use the links above.

International processing

Google, RevenueCat and itch.io may process information outside your country, including in the United States.

Children

The game has crime themes and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete what we hold.
